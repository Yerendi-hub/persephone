# Night of Shadows — host pack

Third game. You page through everything from `/control` (Next / Back); phones and the screen follow. The room votes three times. No player names needed.

## Running order (20 steps)

1. Intro (station, Festival of Shadows) · 2. The cabin (four wounds) · 3–6. Suspects: motive + alibi
7. **Vote 1 — first accusation.** The winner speaks first; then the fixed circle runs: 8–11. four speeches (round 1)
12. **Vote 2 — second accusation.** Same circle, new entry point: 13–16. four speeches (round 2)
17. **Vote 3 — verdict:** any suspect, "None of them" or "All four of them". A tie means "None of them".
18. Verdict reaction · 19. The terminal: the last 15 minutes · 20. The end (all four are murderers)

READ ALOUD always holds the text of the current step. You cannot go back past an opened vote.

## The circle (fixed)

MEDI-7 → Corvin → Ysolde → Seraphine → MEDI-7. Each speaker defends themselves, then accuses the next one with a piece of evidence. Round 1 shows the first half of each piece of evidence, round 2 the second half:

| Accused | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Corvin | Melted belt buckle | Ozone + pocket-blaster burn on the robe |
| Ysolde | Even marks on the throat | Grey ritual dust under the chair |
| Seraphine | Empty venom vial in the carved glowfruit | Bitter almonds in the punch |
| MEDI-7 | Power spike in the terminal log | MEDI-7's cable in the heart stimulator |

Phones keep an "Evidence so far" list for the verdict.

## Solution

All four, in this order: Seraphine (venom → paralysis) · MEDI-7 (malware in the heart stimulator → arrhythmia) · Ysolde (dark power → no air) · Corvin (panic shot → heart stops). None knew about the others.
