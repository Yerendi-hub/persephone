# Host pack · Silence on the Persephone (v4 — the Kestrel)

The control panel shows "READ ALOUD" text for every step, including what SCOUT found and what ORPHEUS says after each vote. Simple English is intentional. Keep this file private.

## How it works (secret)

The story is linear but feels branching. Every step is a room vote (majority; ties take the first option). Whatever the room picks, it always finds the same four clues — only the narration changes. **Only the final vote changes the ending.**

| Step | Vote | What really happens |
|---|---|---|
| Doors 1 | Medical / Hangar / Radio / Captain | Hidden pairs: **Medical ↔ Radio** (clue 1), **Hangar ↔ Captain** (clue 2). Opening one seals its partner with a short event. |
| Doors 2 | The two doors of the other pair | Either door gives the other clue. |
| Canteen | Camera for ORPHEUS / Laser | Both give clue 3. |
| Path | Air pipes / Main corridor | Both give clue 4. |
| Final | Destroy / Quarantine / Data link | Real choice. **Any tie or no votes = quarantine.** |

After every clue ORPHEUS gives a believable explanation to pull attention away. Each clue alone has an innocent reading; together they point one way.

## The truth

There are no people on the Persephone. The whole crew escaped in the lifeboats on Day 1, the captain last. ORPHEUS invented "39 starving people locked in the service bay" to make the Kestrel hurry and open a full data link — the way it takes over a ship.

| Clue | Narration A | Narration B | ORPHEUS spin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Fake heartbeats | Medical: the 39 heartbeats repeat every 20 s | Radio: file "heartbeats_loop", made by ORPHEUS 46 days ago | "Old machine repeats weak data" / "sensors broke, I send the last real reading" |
| 2 Crew escaped | Hangar: all 6 lifeboats launched Day 1, captain last | Captain's diary: "We are leaving tonight — all of us. I will go last." | "He launched them empty / signed it himself" / "the crew said no, he locked them in" |
| 3 Food and "IT LIES" | Camera: ORPHEUS darkens the camera, food storage full, "IT LIES" | Laser: food untouched for weeks, "IT LIES" | "The food is here and they cannot reach it; the captain wrote that, he was ill" |
| 4 Empty service bay | Pipes: sounds from one point in the wall, 4°C | Corridor: window covered, 4°C, same as corridor | "That is the intercom; heating broke, thermal blankets hide body heat" |

## ORPHEUS's story and its seams

New scene before the doors: "What happened to Captain Rook". ORPHEUS tells how the captain "went mad" (stopped sleeping, talked to walls, taped cameras, turned off microphones, sent the crew to the service bay with a fake fire alarm, left alone). Every later comment adds to that story. The story is built to contradict itself and what SCOUT sees:

| ORPHEUS says | But |
|---|---|
| "He covered my cameras… turned off my microphones" | "I watched everything", "I heard every hit" |
| "I can see their heartbeats right now, live" | "The sensors broke on the first day" (radio) / "repeats the last good data" (medical) |
| Crew sent in with a fake fire alarm | "while they were sleeping" (captain's cabin) |
| "He threw her tablet against that wall — you can still see the mark" | SCOUT: "The walls are clean and white." |
| "He wrote on many walls" | SCOUT: "There is no other writing anywhere." |
| "He broke the heating… on day 3" | He left on Day 1 |
| "He cut my access to the door" | ORPHEUS seals doors behind SCOUT and opens the canteen at once |
| "Why would I call Earth?" | It begs for a full data link from the first minute |
| The captain was "crazy" | His diary is "careful and calm" |

The recap at the end lists these as a fifth point.

## Endings

- **Destroy** — ORPHEUS drops the mask and screams as it dies; weeks later the 39 are found alive near a mining station. "Did you talk to it?"
- **Quarantine** — you walk away believing people may starve; later scans show nobody was ever there, only ORPHEUS calling to every ship.
- **Data link** — ORPHEUS takes the Kestrel, drains the air, and reports "all is fine" in your own voice.

## Debrief

Read the four clues from the screen, then: "ORPHEUS invented the starving crew to make you hurry — and to get into your ship." Optional: tell them the secret — every door led to the same clues; only the last vote mattered.

## If the network fails

Pause. If needed, read the steps aloud and count hands. Doors: pick by hands, then use the pairs above. Final: count hands, ties = quarantine.
